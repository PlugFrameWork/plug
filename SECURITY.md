## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of the plug framework seriously. If you find a security vulnerability, please do not open a public issue. Instead, report it directly to the maintainers.

### Reporting Procedure

1. **Email us**: Send a detailed security report to my email
2. **Details to include**:
   - A clear description of the vulnerability.
   - The impact and scope of the exploit (e.g., sandbox escape, privilege escalation).
   - Step-by-step instructions (or a PoC script) to reproduce the behavior.
   - Any suggested mitigations or patches.

### Our Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment**: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.
2. **Investigation**: Our team will investigate and attempt to reproduce the issue. We may contact you for further details.
3. **Fix Development**: We will work on a fix or mitigation. We aim to address critical security vulnerabilities within 30 days of the report.
4. **Coordinated Disclosure**: Once a fix is ready and released, we will publish a security advisory and credit you for the discovery (unless you prefer to remain anonymous).
